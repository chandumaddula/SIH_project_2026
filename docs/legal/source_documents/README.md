# Authoritative Source Documents Repository

This directory contains reference copies of gazette notifications, primary statutes, and statutory rules governing Legal Metrology in India:

1. **The Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (No. 1 of 2010)**: Primary parliamentary statute.
2. **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (G.S.R. 202(E))**: Core rules for packaged commodities.
3. **Advisory Notes & Department Guidelines**: Directives issued by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, Government of India.
