# Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 & Amendments Summary

## 1. Overview
The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, framed under Section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, regulate the packaging, labeling, net quantity, pricing, and mandatory declarations on pre-packaged commodities sold in India. Amendments in 2021, 2022, and 2024 introduced enhanced digital disclosures, unit sale price mandates, and strict rules for e-commerce entities.

## 2. Core Mandatory Declarations (Rule 6)
Every package containing a commodity shall bear thereon, on the principal display panel or outer container, legible and prominent declarations of:

1. **Name and Address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer** (Rule 6(1)(a)):
   - Complete legal name and registered office address with PIN code.
   - For imported goods, the country of origin and importer details must be clearly specified.

2. **Generic or Common Name of Commodity** (Rule 6(1)(b)):
   - Unambiguous identity of the packaged product.

3. **Net Quantity** (Rule 6(1)(c)):
   - Net weight or volume expressed in standard units of measurement (S.I. units: `g`, `kg`, `mL`, `l` or `L`, `N` or `m`, `cm`, `sq m`).
   - Non-standard units (e.g. `gm`, `grms`, `ltrs`, `kgs`, `doz`) are non-compliant.

4. **Month and Year of Manufacture / Packing / Import** (Rule 6(1)(d)):
   - Stated in `MM/YYYY` or `Month YYYY` format (e.g., `06/2024` or `June 2024`).

5. **Maximum Retail Price (MRP)** (Rule 6(1)(e)):
   - Expressed as: `MRP Rs XX.XX (inclusive of all taxes)` or `Maximum Retail Price ₹ XX.XX (inclusive of all taxes)`.
   - Dual pricing on identical packages is strictly prohibited under Rule 18(2A).

6. **Unit Sale Price** (Amendment 2021, Rule 6(1)(11)):
   - For packages > 1 kg/1 L, price per kg/L; for packages < 1 kg/1 L, price per 100g/100mL or per unit.

7. **Consumer Care Details** (Rule 6(1)(f)):
   - Name, address, telephone number, and email ID of the person/office to contact in case of consumer complaints.

## 3. Font Size & Legibility Standards (Rule 9 & Schedule II)
- Height of numerals and letters in mandatory declarations depends on Principal Display Panel (PDP) area.

## 4. Key Exemptions (Rule 26 & Rule 3)
1. Small packages containing net weight/volume of $\le 10 \text{ g}$ or $\le 10 \text{ mL}$.
2. Packages sold to industrial consumers or institutional consumers (Rule 3).
3. Agricultural commodities packaged in quantities $> 50 \text{ kg}$.
